# Chachan — Android beta

Tap your Ray-Ban / Bluetooth glasses and your own ChatGPT answers by voice, hands-free,
with the phone still in your pocket.

**Install:** grab the APK from [Releases](../../releases/latest).

- Android 8.0+ and the ChatGPT app installed and signed in
- No microphone permission, no account, collects nothing
- Open the app once to finish setup, then just tap your glasses
- After about a minute of quiet the conversation ends on its own — tap again to start a new one
- Take the glasses off to end

Distribution repo only — the app source lives elsewhere.
